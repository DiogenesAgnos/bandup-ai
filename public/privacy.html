Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact form submissions (name, email, country, age group, message)
  • Essay content submitted for analysis
  • Payment information (processed and stored by Paddle — we do not store card details)
  • Usage data collected via Google Analytics (anonymised)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: provide and improve the Service; process subscription payments; respond to your enquiries; send service-related communications; and analyse usage patterns to improve user experience.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Essay Data

Essays you submit are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI API for analysis. Essays are transmitted securely and are not stored permanently on our servers. We do not use your essays to train AI models. Please refer to Anthropic's Privacy Policy for details on their data handling practices.

4. Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymised data about how users interact with our Service. This includes pages visited, time spent, and general geographic location. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data is transmitted over HTTPS encryption.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide the Service and comply with legal obligations. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 2 years.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your personal data; correct inaccurate data; request deletion of your data; withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at diogenes.agnos@gmail.com.

8. Third-Party Services

Our Service integrates with: Anthropic Claude API (essay analysis); Paddle (payment processing); Google Analytics (usage analytics); EmailJS (contact form delivery). Each third party has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

9. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, contact us at: diogenes.agnos@gmail.com
