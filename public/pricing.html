No. BandUp AI is an AI-powered preparation tool. Actual IELTS scores are determined solely by certified IELTS examiners. Our scores are for practice and guidance only.

BandUp AI supports IELTS Writing Task 2 (essay), Task 1 Academic (graphs/charts), and Task 1 General Training (letters) — all with full AI feedback.
